Curriculum & Programs

Academics at Highridge School

Highridge School follows a practical, child-centered approach to learning that supports nursery, pre-primary, and primary education with a focus on academic achievement, inquiry, and life skills.

r

Learning Stages

Our educational stages provide a smooth progression from baby classes and nursery to primary class 7, while supporting both academic development and personal growth.

Play Group

Safe, joyful routines designed to nurture early social skills, communication, motor coordination, and curiosity in toddlers.

Pre-School

Hands-on early literacy, counting, creative arts, and discovery-based learning that instill reading confidence and teamwork.

Primary Grades 1-6

Strong foundations through structured national curriculum subjects, regular assessment, STEM enrichment, and exam preparation.

Pillars

Curriculum Overview

We ensure a balanced curriculum that fosters intellect, digital fluency, creativity, and moral integrity.

Literacy & Numeracy Mastery

English, Kiswahili, and Mathematics taught through interactive problem solving, daily reading routines, and practical application.

Science & STEM Innovations

Hands-on science experiments and basic technology/coding introduction to ignite curiosity and analytical thinking.

Values & Character Education

Dedicated programs that teach empathy, honesty, personal responsibility, teamwork, and active community citizenship.

Language & Cultural Pride

Bilingual proficiency and cultural heritage appreciation to cultivate confident self-expression and global awareness.

Academic Year Schedule

Our academic year is organized into structured terms that balance rigorous learning with constructive rest.

Term 1

January to May: Orientation, diagnostic assessment, foundation building, and classroom routines.

Term 2

July to November: Core subject mastery, STEM projects, midterm reviews, and creative competitions.
