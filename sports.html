Athletics & Clubs

Sports Academy

Highridge School believes that sports are a powerful tool for physical fitness, discipline, self-confidence, and social integration, especially for girls and underprivileged students.

Visit Sports Academy

Sports Programs

Our dedicated sports programmes encourage discipline, healthy competition, leadership, and teamwork while creating pathways for long-term personal growth.

Soccer Academy

Technical footwork, teamwork strategies, friendly inter-school matches, and physical endurance training.

Basketball

Dribbling drills, shooting techniques, spatial awareness, and fast-paced team coordination.

Swimming & Track Athletics

Water safety, swimming stroke mastery, sprint races, long jump, and athletic endurance building.

Sports Academy Vision

Vision for the Sports Academy

To inspire and nurture the physical, mental, and emotional well-being of young athletes, fostering leadership, excellence, and social equity through sports, while creating pathways for lifelong success and community engagement.

Infrastructure

Develop and maintain quality facilities for football, basketball, athletics, swimming and multi-use courts.

Talent Development

Introduce structured programmes for recreational and competitive athletes, guided by professional coaches.

Inclusion & Girls' Empowerment

Design initiatives to increase girls’ participation, mentorship programmes, and scholarships for promising athletes.

Strategic Goals

  1. Build inclusive, accessible sports infrastructure.
  2. Integrate sports with education and wellness.
  3. Create pathways to higher-level competition and scholarship opportunities.
  4. Strengthen community partnerships and sponsorships.
