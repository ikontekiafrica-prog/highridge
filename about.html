Nature of the School

About Highridge School

Highridge School provides pre-primary and primary education. The school is co-educational and places special emphasis on enrolling children from underprivileged families, with a growing focus on increasing girls' access to education.

Levels of Education

We offer pre-primary (nursery and baby classes) and seven years of primary education (Classes 1–7). Baby classes are also available for younger children (ages 2–4) to help with early socialisation and foundational skills.

Location

Highridge School is located in the Oloirien area, south-eastern Arusha, on plot number 167A/167D, next to the P.P.F housing complex and near the (now defunct) General Tyre factory. The school is approximately 400 meters from the tarmac road off the Town–Njiro road and is accessible year-round.

Premises & Classrooms

The campus covers 5,559 square meters and currently houses 18 classrooms in addition to offices, dining facilities, and toilets.

Founders & History

Highridge School was founded by Mr. and Mrs. Washokera in 2000 with just five pupils. Within two years the pre-primary programme expanded and the primary school was established to provide a continuous learning pathway for pupils.

Mission

The school's mission is to create an enabling environment where students acquire knowledge and skills through routine, hands-on, practical-based classroom instruction delivered by committed, qualified teachers.

Vision

To provide leadership in quality pre-primary and primary education that promotes skills for self-reliance and national development.

Policies & Approach

Highridge focuses on hiring committed teachers, providing on-the-job teacher training (including international volunteer trainers), supporting underprivileged children through sponsorships, and giving special preference to girls to address gender imbalances.

Why Invest in Highridge

  • Increase standards of primary education through high-quality teaching.
  • Provide accessible education to needy families via affordable fees and sponsorships.
  • Use sports and extracurriculars to develop holistic skills and community engagement.
